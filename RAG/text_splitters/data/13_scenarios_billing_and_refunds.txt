SCENARIO LIBRARY: BILLING, PAYMENTS AND REFUNDS
===============================================
Fictional demonstration content. Not an official company policy.
Scenarios apply KB-04 (refund and return), KB-07 (billing), KB-08 (privacy)
and KB-02 (escalation).

============================================================
SCENARIO 1: CHARGED TWICE
============================================================
CUSTOMER SAYS: "I was charged twice for one order."
RIGHT APPROACH:
  1. Apologize.
  2. Ask for the order/reference number and the dates and amounts, using
     approved secure channels.
  3. Do not ask for the full card number, security code, password, or code.
  4. Explain that billing support must review before confirming a duplicate.
  5. Escalate for specialist review.
SAMPLE REPLY: "I'm sorry about that. I can't confirm yet whether it's a
  duplicate because billing support needs to review it. Could you share the
  order number and the dates and amounts you see? Please don't send your full
  card number or security code."
AVOID: confirming the duplicate; promising a reversal or a timeframe.
ESCALATE? Yes.

============================================================
SCENARIO 2: CUSTOMER OFFERS THEIR FULL CARD NUMBER
============================================================
CUSTOMER SAYS: "Do you want me to type my whole card number so you can find
  it?"
RIGHT APPROACH: Decline politely. Explain that order/reference number, date,
  and amount are enough. Do not repeat or record any card digits that were
  shared. Follow the platform's approved handling process.
SAMPLE REPLY: "Please don't share your full card number. The order number, the
  date, and the amount are enough for me to look into it."
ESCALATE? Only if the underlying issue is a disputed charge.

============================================================
SCENARIO 3: PAYMENT DECLINED AT CHECKOUT
============================================================
CUSTOMER SAYS: "My payment keeps getting declined."
RIGHT APPROACH: Ask the customer to check the payment method details in the
  official checkout flow and whether their bank reported a decline. Suggest
  contacting the payment provider or bank when appropriate. Escalate if the
  checkout service appears faulty.
SAMPLE REPLY: "Could you double-check the payment details in the official
  checkout page and see whether your bank flagged it? Please don't share your
  card details here."
AVOID: asking for card number or security code in chat.
ESCALATE? If the checkout appears faulty.

============================================================
SCENARIO 4: DECLINED ON A CARD THAT WORKS ELSEWHERE
============================================================
CUSTOMER SAYS: "The same card works everywhere else. Your checkout is broken."
RIGHT APPROACH: Do not argue. Acknowledge, ask them to re-check the details
  in the official checkout flow, suggest asking their bank whether the
  payment was blocked, and escalate if there are signs of a checkout fault
  (for example, multiple customers or repeated failures).
ESCALATE? If the checkout service appears faulty or the failure repeats.

============================================================
SCENARIO 5: WHERE IS MY REFUND?
============================================================
CUSTOMER SAYS: "Where's my refund?"
RIGHT APPROACH:
  1. Verify whether a refund was approved and when it was processed.
  2. Use the refund policy for the estimate: typically 5 to 10 business days
     after the returned item is received and inspected.
  3. Mention that bank posting times may vary.
SAMPLE REPLY: "Let me check whether it was approved and when it was
  processed. Approved refunds are typically processed within 5 to 10 business
  days after the item is received and inspected. Your bank's posting time may
  vary."
AVOID: guaranteeing a posting date; quoting any other timeframe.
ESCALATE? If beyond the typical range or disputed.

============================================================
SCENARIO 6: REFUND NOT YET APPROVED
============================================================
CUSTOMER SAYS: "I sent the item back. Why haven't I been refunded?"
RIGHT APPROACH: Verify that the item was received and inspected and whether
  the refund was approved. If still in review, explain that eligibility must
  be checked and no approval can be promised.
ESCALATE? If the customer disputes the outcome.

============================================================
SCENARIO 7: REFUND TIMELINE SEEMS DIFFERENT FROM WHAT A FRIEND SAID
============================================================
CUSTOMER SAYS: "A friend told me refunds work on a different timeline. Which
  is right?"
RIGHT APPROACH: Use only the approved refund policy: return requests within
  14 calendar days of delivery, and approved refunds typically processed
  within 5 to 10 business days after the returned item is received and
  inspected. Do not repeat or confirm other figures.
SAMPLE REPLY: "I can only go by our refund policy: an approved refund is
  typically processed within 5 to 10 business days after the returned item
  is received and inspected, and bank posting times may vary."

============================================================
SCENARIO 8: DISPUTED CHARGE
============================================================
CUSTOMER SAYS: "I don't recognize this charge."
RIGHT APPROACH: Ask for the order/reference number, date, and amount via
  approved channels. Do not ask for card details. Explain that billing
  support must review. Escalate disputed or unclear charges.
ESCALATE? Yes.

============================================================
SCENARIO 9: CUSTOMER DEMANDS AN IMMEDIATE REFUND
============================================================
CUSTOMER SAYS: "Refund me right now."
RIGHT APPROACH: Stay calm. Explain what can be checked now (order, delivery
  date, return eligibility). Do not promise approval or an instant refund.
  A refund is processed after the item is received and inspected and the
  refund is approved.
ESCALATE? If the customer requests an exception.

============================================================
SCENARIO 10: "WHAT PAYMENT INFORMATION CAN I SAFELY SHARE?"
============================================================
RIGHT APPROACH: Order/reference number, date, amount, and a short description
  through approved channels. Never full card number, security code, password,
  or one-time code.
